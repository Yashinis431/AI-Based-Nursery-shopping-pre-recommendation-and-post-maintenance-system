🌿 Plant Details

Learn about each plant before you buy

Snake Plant

  • Air purifying plant
  • Survives low light
  • Water once in 10–14 days
  • Best for bedroom & office
  • Removes toxins from air

Money Plant

  • Grows in soil & water
  • Needs medium sunlight
  • Water weekly
  • Fast growing plant
  • Brings positive energy

Croton

  • Colorful indoor plant
  • Needs bright indirect light
  • Water when soil feels dry
  • Best for living room
  • Adds vibrant color indoors

Rose

  • Flowering plant
  • Needs full sunlight
  • Daily watering required
  • Needs pruning
  • Beautiful fragrance flowers

Aloe Vera

  • Medicinal plant
  • Needs very less water
  • Grows in bright sunlight
  • Skin healing gel inside leaves
  • Very low maintenance

Sunflower

  • Bright outdoor flowering plant
  • Needs full direct sunlight
  • Water 2–3 times a week
  • Grows very tall & fast
  • Attracts bees & butterflies

Hibiscus

  • Beautiful flowering shrub
  • Needs medium sunlight
  • Water daily in summer
  • Needs regular pruning
  • Best for experienced gardeners

Marigold

  • Easy to grow outdoors
  • Needs medium sunlight
  • Water every 2 days
  • Repels insects naturally
  • Perfect for beginners

Zinnia

  • Colorful flowering plant
  • Needs medium sunlight
  • Water when topsoil dries
  • Grows quickly from seeds
  • Great for beginner gardeners
